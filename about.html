Company Profile

Engineering the Future

Defining excellence in infrastructure through precision, scale, and unwavering commitment to industrial progress.

"Growth is Life"

AHA Engineering is a premier engineering company and the largest private sector corporation in India

Our motto "Growth is Life" captures the ever-evolving spirit of AHA Engineering.

In just over four decades, we have evolved into one of India's most valuable and stakeholder-centric enterprises. We have built world-class assets, driven innovation at scale, and contributed meaningfully to India's growth story.

AHA Engineering's activities span hydrocarbon exploration and production, petroleum refining and marketing, petrochemicals, renewables (solar and hydrogen), heavy infrastructure development, and industrial manufacturing.
